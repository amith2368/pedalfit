# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-19

First release.

- Side-view video analysis with MediaPipe Pose (heavy / full models), fully offline
- Knee, hip, back, shoulder, elbow and ankle angles; knee-over-pedal; hip and shoulder movement
- Target windows for 8 bike types × 3 fit goals, adjusted for mobility and pain
- Saddle height / setback and bar reach / stack recommendations in millimetres
- Key-position snapshots, motion replay, joint tracking paths, angle chart
- Stem calculator, fit history, print to PDF
- Webcam recording and photo-based straight-leg-raise measurement
