Ride video

How to film

  1. Put your bike on a trainer (or use a smart / spin bike) on level ground.
  2. Camera side-on, square to the bike, at hip height, 2.5–4 m away, in landscape.
  3. Whole body, bike and both wheels in frame. Good light, fitted clothing, no baggy shorts.
  4. Ride in your normal position (hoods for road, aero bars for TT) at a steady 80–90 rpm with some resistance.
  5. Record at least 15 seconds once you're warmed up.
